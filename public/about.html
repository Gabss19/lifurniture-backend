Who We Are

Crafted with Heart,
Built to Last

LiFurniture began in 2015 as a small family-run carpentry workshop in Valenzuela City. What started as a passion for precision woodworking has grown into a trusted name for custom furniture across Metro Manila. Every piece we build carries the same dedication and personal attention we put into our very first project.

We specialize in made-to-order cabinets, drawer units, and built-in storage solutions. Unlike mass-produced furniture, every item we create begins with a proper site visit — we come to your home, measure your space, listen to your needs, and design something that fits perfectly, both physically and aesthetically.

Our workshop uses solid hardwood, quality plyboard, and durable hardware selected for long-term performance. We believe furniture should not just look good — it should be built to withstand daily life for years to come. That commitment to quality, from the first consultation through final installation, is what sets us apart.

What We Use

Premium Materials

We source only trusted, locally available materials chosen for durability, workability, and appearance. From solid Philippine hardwood to engineered boards, every material is selected with your furniture's long-term performance in mind.

Narra Wood

A Philippine hardwood prized for its rich reddish-brown tone and natural durability. Ideal for premium drawer units and accent pieces.

Acacia Wood

Locally sourced, dense, and highly resistant to moisture. A popular choice for kitchen cabinets and high-use storage units.

Marine Plywood

Moisture-resistant and structurally stable. Commonly used for under-sink cabinets and areas exposed to humidity.

Melamine Board

A cost-effective engineered board with a smooth, easy-to-clean surface. Available in various finishes for modern cabinetry.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a typical project take?

+

Project timelines vary depending on complexity and materials. A standard cabinet unit typically takes 2–3 weeks from design approval to installation. Larger or more complex projects (such as full kitchen sets or multi-room builds) may take 4–6 weeks. We'll give you an accurate timeline after the site visit.

Is the site visit really free?

+

Yes, absolutely. The initial consultation and site visit — including measurements and a preliminary design discussion — are completely free of charge with no obligation to proceed. We only begin charging once you formally approve the design and quote.

What areas do you serve?

+

Our workshop is based in Valenzuela City, and we serve all areas within Metro Manila including Quezon City, Caloocan, Malabon, Navotas, and surrounding cities. For areas outside Metro Manila, please reach out to us directly and we'll see what we can arrange.

Can I choose my own materials or finish?

+

Definitely. Part of our consultation process is discussing your material preferences, color or finish options, and hardware choices. We'll present you with samples and guide you toward the best options for your budget and space.

Do you offer a warranty on your work?

+

Yes. We stand behind the quality of our craftsmanship. All our completed installations come with a workmanship warranty covering structural issues and installation defects. Details are included in your project agreement. We're always available for after-service support as well.

What is your payment arrangement?

+

We typically work on a milestone-based payment structure: a downpayment upon project approval, a progress payment when production begins, and the final balance upon installation completion. Full payment terms are discussed and agreed upon before any work begins.
